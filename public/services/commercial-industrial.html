What does Grando Solar do?

Grando Solar is a solar EPC company in Gujarat, a brand of Om Shanti Machineries Exim. We design, install and maintain rooftop, ground-mounted and carport solar plants for homes, housing societies, commercial buildings and industries, with offices in Surat, Vadodara, Vapi, Bharuch and Ahmedabad.

How much subsidy can I get on rooftop solar in Gujarat?

Under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, residential systems of 3 kW and above are eligible for ₹78,000 central subsidy. Housing societies and RWAs can get ₹18,000 per kW for common-meter installations. Commercial and industrial systems can claim accelerated depreciation instead. Grando Solar files the complete application for you.

How does an on-grid solar system work?

Panels convert sunlight into DC power, the inverter converts it to AC for your load, and a bi-directional net meter records what you export to the grid and import from it. Your bill is calculated on the net units.

Is the structure safe in heavy wind and monsoon?

Yes. Grando's storm-safe structures are pre-fabricated from HDGI virgin steel with SS304 nut-bolts and Hilti RE10 chemical anchoring. No cutting or welding happens on site, so every joint is factory-consistent and rust-proof.

Can solar damage my roof or cause leakage?

No. We use Hilti chemical anchoring with an airtight seal, and elevated designs where the roof needs to stay usable. Our engineers inspect the site on the free visit before recommending a mounting method.

Do you offer loans or EMI for business installations?

Yes. Grando Solar assists with loan facility through financing partners, and businesses can also benefit from accelerated depreciation to improve payback further.

Do I need to clean the panels?

A simple water wash every few weeks keeps dust off and generation high. Grando Solar offers AMC packages that include scheduled cleaning and preventive checks for business installations.
